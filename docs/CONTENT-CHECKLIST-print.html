Website Content — What To Collect

Everything below is a real field on alammana.pk. Items marked Optional are simply hidden on the page when missing, so send what you have and we add the rest later. It does not all have to arrive at once.

Send everything to me on WhatsApp. One folder or message per project, named after the project. If you are unsure about anything on this list, ask before guessing — a wrong detail on the site is worse than a missing one.

Photo rules — applies to everything

  • Landscape / horizontal wherever possible. Portrait phone photos get cropped badly on wide screens.
  • Minimum 1600px wide. Straight from the phone camera is perfect.
  • Send as “Document” in WhatsApp, not as “Photo”. Sending as a Photo compresses the image and we lose quality. This one matters.
  • Do not send screenshots of photos.
  • No watermarks, and no other company’s phone number or logo in the frame.
  • Daylight, camera held level, whole building in frame for elevations.
  • Name files simply: faisal-jewel-progress-mar-2026-1.jpg
